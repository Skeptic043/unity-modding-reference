# Temporary field changes and restoration

When a patch scales a native rate for one callback, per-invocation capture and restoration prevents normal, skipped, exceptional or repeated calls from compounding the temporary value.

[HarmonyX](README.md)

## Invocation scope

The applicable callback surface includes the consumption formula, visual and depletion duties, and possible reentrancy. The described HarmonyX pattern carries original state through the `HarmonyPrefix` and `__state`, with a capture marker distinguishing a finalizer reached without capture.

Restoration occurs on normal and exceptional exits while preserving the original `__exception`. Prefix skipping and finalizer order remain specific to the installed HarmonyX implementation rather than another Harmony fork or version.

## Value and wrapper behavior

Exercise zero, identity, faster and slower values, nonfinite values, and extreme accepted inputs through production scaling and restoration code. Check that repeated calls do not compound. Distinguish direct helper invocation from Harmony's generated wrapper.

In game, compare equal simulation intervals, depletion and refill, other callback responsibilities, save and reload, and coinstalled patches. Single-precision resolution and rounded UI can hide small changes.

Synthetic checks can establish the calculation and restoration branches. They do not establish runtime ordering, reentrancy safety, visuals, persistence or another patch's interaction.

The [complete verification recipe](../../evidence/methods/verify-temporary-rate-field-patch.md) covers inspection, synthetic restoration checks and live acceptance separately.
